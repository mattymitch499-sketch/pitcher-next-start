## 2026-07-17 — Explain the decision and expose model freshness clearly

- A list of model drivers is not enough: the player page must explicitly synthesize why the projection is favorable or risky, what factors support it, and what factors create downside.
- Do not present an artifact identifier as primary dashboard information. Show a human-readable training cutoff/freshness state, with the technical ID available as secondary provenance.
- Before calling a season model operational, compare the artifact cutoff with the selected slate/season. A prior-season artifact may be valid structurally but is stale for in-season use and must be refreshed or visibly flagged.
- When adding sections to an established editorial theme, inspect the rendered result and reuse its visual grammar. Do not introduce a large dark dashboard panel whose contrast, density, and tone conflict with the surrounding light page.
